# CORTEX — S2 Market Requirements Document

**Date:** 2026-04-08
**Author:** Consultant (Claude Chat)
**Product:** CORTEX — Visual Capture & OCR Knowledge Tool (iOS)
**Note:** Post-pivot. Text/URL lane ceded to Readwise Reader. CORTEX = visual/OCR lane only.

---

## 1. Problem Statement

Knowledge workers and students capture text digitally (Readwise, Notion) but have no good tool for capturing knowledge from the physical world — whiteboards, conference slides, recipe cards, handwritten notes, business cards. Apple Notes scans documents but doesn't structure or tag them. Microsoft Lens retired in January 2026, leaving a gap.

## 2. Market

**TAM:** 200M+ smartphone users who photograph whiteboards/notes
**SAM:** iOS users who want structured visual capture — ~10M
**SOM (12-month):** 1,000-5,000 users via App Store organic (Microsoft Lens refugees)
**Key event:** Microsoft Lens retired Jan 2026 — millions of users seeking alternatives

## 3. Customer

**Primary: The Knowledge Worker** — photographs whiteboards after meetings, conference slides, business cards. Wants: OCR → structured, tagged, searchable item. Not just a photo dump.

**Secondary: The Student** — photographs lecture notes, textbook pages, lab diagrams. Wants: organized by subject, searchable during study sessions.

## 4. Competition

| Competitor | Threat | Gap for CORTEX |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| Apple Notes Scanner | 🔴 High (free) | No structured tagging or knowledge graph |
| Google Lens | 🔴 High (free, AI) | No persistence — scan and forget |
| Adobe Scan | 🟡 Medium | PDF-focused, not knowledge-focused |
| Evernote | 🟡 Medium (declining) | Bloated, $14.99/mo |
| Readwise | Ally (not competitor) | Text-only — we own the visual lane |

**Positioning:** CORTEX = Visual Readwise. Readwise captures digital text. CORTEX captures physical-world knowledge.

## 5. Pricing

- Free: 5 captures/month
- Pro: $3.99/mo (Apple IAP)

## 6. Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Captures per active user/week | ≥3 |
| OCR accuracy | ≥85% |
| D7 retention | ≥35% |
| Free → Pro conversion | ≥8% |
